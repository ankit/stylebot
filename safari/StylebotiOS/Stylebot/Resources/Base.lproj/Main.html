Stylebot

Change how any website looks in Safari.

Turn on Stylebot in Safari

  1. Open any website in *Safari*.
  2. Tap the page menu on the left of the address bar, then *Manage Extensions*, and turn on *Stylebot*.
  3. Tap the extensions button in the address bar and turn on *Stylebot*.
  4. When Safari asks for access to websites, allow it on every website.
Open Safari

Then open Stylebot from the same menu to restyle the page.
Or turn it on in Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions.
