Stylebot

Change how any website looks in Safari.

Turn on Stylebot in Safari

  1. Tap the page menu (left of the address bar), then *Manage Extensions*, and turn on *Stylebot*.
  2. Tap the extensions button and turn on *Stylebot*.
  3. Allow it on every website.
  4. Open Stylebot and try a style from the *Gallery*.
  5. Open Stylebot and try a style from the *Gallery*, or style the page yourself.
Open Safari

Or turn it on in Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions.
