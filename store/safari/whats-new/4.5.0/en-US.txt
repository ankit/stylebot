The editor's Basic tab is easier to use, with a box diagram for margin, padding and size, and new controls for italic, letter case and borders on some sides only. After you pick something, choose whether a change applies to just that element, similar ones, or all of them. The Code tab has new color themes, and you can move and resize the editor. The Presets tab is gone: turn on Readability from the popup, and grayscale from Effects. This update also fixes some pages turning white with no style saved.
